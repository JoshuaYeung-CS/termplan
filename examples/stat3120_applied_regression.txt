1. COURSE DESCRIPTION
Linear models for real data: simple and multiple regression, inference for coefficients,
diagnostics, transformations, model selection, logistic regression and an introduction to
mixed models. Every topic is taught through a data analysis in R.

Prerequisite: STAT 2100 or an equivalent introductory statistics course.

2. TEXTS
- Course notes (free PDF on the course site), referred to below as "the Notes".
- Optional: any recent edition of a standard regression textbook.
